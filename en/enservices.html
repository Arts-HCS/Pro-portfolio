SERVICE DETAILS

Price

I offer my services FREE of charge! As a young developer, I'm looking to gain experience through new projects. The best payment you can give me is your recommendation.

Delivery

Once the service is confirmed, the delivery date is set based on the size of the website. This timeframe can range from two to four weeks. However, it's an estimate and any changes will be communicated in advance.

Hosting

After one week of testing following the delivery of your website, you have the option to host it online. This service is priced separately from the website itself, so it comes at an additional cost.

Warranty

After the website is delivered, the client receives one week of testing with the freedom to request minor changes or report performance issues. After this week, a 30-day warranty begins, covering the minor details listed below. If the client requires changes beyond what the warranty covers, a maintenance service will apply.

Color Font type Background
Content Size Images
Animation Position Dropdown menus

Maintenance

If the client requires changes that go beyond the warranty's coverage, they have two options:

Annual fee Service fee
A start date is agreed upon, and a fee of MXN $2,000 is paid, covering up to five changes for a full year. If the limit is exceeded, it will be charged as a service fee. A payment of MXN $500 is made for each change requested.
